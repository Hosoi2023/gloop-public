**Competitive Analysis**

**IMDB.com**
Weaknesses:
Unclear homepage content: Movies are shown without explanation. Feel random & I question whether it’s advertising.
Lacks human-driven content.
Poor content prioritisation: “Top 10 on IMDB” is buried too low. Important content isn’t surfaced early.
Strengths:
Strong visual design: Black background highlights movie posters and yellow font accents emphasises key information clearly. Good contrast and readability.

**rottentomatoes.com**
Weaknesses: 
Inefficient use of space: Doesn’t use full screen width & feels constrained.
Distracting homepage elements: slideshow with gossip/irrelevant content. Reduces focus on core purpose (movies & ratings).
Confusing rating system: Multiple scores (tomatometer vs popcorn meter) with no clear explanation of the differences. Visually and conceptually unclear.
Strengths
Gave me the idea of making a page for events/awards such as movies at the Oscars, Festivale de Cannes, etc.) But I would want to keep it factual and list-like as much as possible. No fluff.

**Letterboxd.com**
Weaknesses
Narrow layout
5-star rating system lacks granularity. Doesn’t distinguish between close ratings. I prefer a rating out of 100.

**criticker.com**
Weaknesses
Strengths
Clear and consistent design like IMDB. Also feels modern. Makes full use of screen space.
Strong value proposition: the homepage clearly explains the purpose of the platform, how the recommendations work. The ratings are user driven.
Excellent onboarding clarity: Shows examples (pictures) of what recommendations and collections will look like. Uses familiar films which builds trust.
Trending section
Efficient logged-in experience: Grid of films, quick actions (rate, add to list). There are no distractions or forced elements.
Gave me the idea of including which streaming platform the film is available to watch on

**tastedive.com**
Very poor visual design
Lack of clarity. Feels inauthentic. No explanation for why certain films are shown. Homepage content lacks meaning or logic.

**movielens.org**
No trust or credibility. Page feels empty and unfinished.
Poor design quality. Narrow page, unattractive colours. 
Lack of polish. No capital letters. Doesn’t feel professional or reliable.

**Summary**
Clarity & purpose are critical
Human-centered content
Design = trust
Ratings must be simple and precise
Remove noise, focus on action

**Product Direction**
A clean, modern, human-driven film platform that prioritises clarity, trust and instant interaction. Users log, rate and discover movies without noise, confusion or artificial recommendations.

**Key features:**
Watch journal (log, rate, comment, recommend/avoid
Where to stream
Trending content
Grid-based, action first homepage
